Structured Delivery With Humans in Control

Guided AI workflows for software delivery, architecture, and security — with your team firmly in the driver's seat

Cosmogony is a collaborative AI platform that helps teams take ideas from initial business need to verified results—without taking over your process, your infrastructure, or your judgment.

Rather than acting as an unpredictable autocomplete tool, Cosmogony coordinates specialist AI teammates across clearly defined stages. Every stage produces a clean, readable document saved directly to your project branch, and pauses at a checkpoint so your team stays in control.

The Six-Stage Software Delivery Pipeline (RADPI)

Stage 1

Requirements

Turns your brief, idea, or GitHub Issue into clear goals, user stories, and acceptance criteria.

Stage 2

Analysis

Explores your existing project to understand how the change fits in and highlights any risks early.

Stage 3

Design

Shapes a clear technical design that aligns with your team's patterns before any code is written.

Stage 4

Planning

Breaks the agreed design into a clear, step-by-step action plan so delivery stays predictable.

Stage 5

Implementation

Builds the solution step by step on an isolated feature branch while keeping your main branch untouched.

Stage 6

Verification

Automatically runs builds and quality checks to prove the solution works before handing it back to you.

Every stage saves its deliverable to your repository branch so nothing is ever lost and every decision is easy to review and audit.

More Than Code: Architecture, Security & Custom Workflows

Modern product delivery involves more than just writing code. Cosmogony includes ready-made workflows for architecture and security teams, plus a visual studio for creating your own:

  • Enterprise Architecture (BAC4): Guides business analysts and architects from Business Needs and Business Analysis through User Stories and full C4 Architecture documentation.
  • Security Threat Modeling (STARC): Helps security and engineering teams map system scope, identify potential attack surfaces, evaluate risks, and define clear countermeasures.
  • Custom Workflow Studio: Allows organisation administrators to design, customize, and publish versioned workflows tailored to their own playbooks, review standards, and specialist AI roles.

Human Checkpoints & Effortless Rework

After each stage, the workflow pauses and presents a clean summary for your review. You can approve with one click to move forward, leave feedback to refine the current stage, or rewind to any earlier stage if priorities shift.

Flexible control for every task: Want human sign-off at every milestone? Keep standard checkpoints enabled. Running a routine task or rapid prototype? Turn on Auto-Approve to let the workflow run end-to-end automatically.

Verified Results, Not Guesswork

"Done" in Cosmogony means the work has actually been built, tested, and verified in a clean workspace—not just that an AI claims it finished.

  • Automated quality checks confirm that the project builds cleanly and passes tests before the final checkpoint completes.
  • If a step runs into an issue, built-in One-Click Diagnostics pinpoint the cause and recommend the fastest way to resolve it.
  • A complete Workflow History lets you inspect every stage run and document revision at any time.

Collaborate Across Web, VS Code, Terminal & Mobile

Multiple teammates can join the same live session to follow progress, read stage documents, and collaborate in real time. Start a session from the Web dashboard, work alongside your code in the VS Code extension or 3-column Terminal UI, and review deliverables or approve checkpoints on the go from the Android mobile app.
