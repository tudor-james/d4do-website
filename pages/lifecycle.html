Structured Delivery With Humans in Control

Guided AI workflows for software delivery, architecture, and security — with your team firmly in the driver's seat

Cosmogony is a collaborative AI platform that helps teams take ideas from initial business need to verified results—without taking over your process, your infrastructure, or your judgment.

Rather than acting as an unpredictable autocomplete tool, Cosmogony coordinates specialist AI teammates inside a shared session. Every milestone produces a clean, readable deliverable saved to an isolated feature branch, and pauses at a checkpoint so your team stays in control.

How Delivery Flows

More Than Code: Software, Architecture & Security

Modern product delivery involves more than just writing code. Cosmogony provides ready-made workflows for your whole organisation, plus a visual studio for creating your own:

  • Software Delivery: Takes feature requests and bug reports from initial brief to tested, working code on a dedicated feature branch.
  • Enterprise Architecture: Helps business analysts and architects turn high-level goals into structured system and C4 architecture documentation.
  • Security Threat Modeling: Guides teams in identifying risks, mapping attack surfaces, and defining clear security countermeasures.
  • Custom Workflow Studio: Allows administrators to design, version, and publish custom workflows tailored to their own playbooks and review standards.

Human Checkpoints & Effortless Refinement

At key milestones, the workflow pauses and presents a clean summary for your review. You can approve with one click to move forward, or give feedback to refine the work before proceeding.

Flexible control for every task: Want human sign-off at every milestone? Keep standard checkpoints enabled. Running a routine task or rapid prototype? Turn on Auto-Approve to let the workflow run end-to-end automatically.

Self-Correcting Loops & Continuous Team Learning

Cosmogony is not a static prompt wrapper. Its workflows are built around self-correcting verification loops and adaptive Efficiency Tokenomics that continuously learn how your team works—without ever needing to retrain or fine-tune underlying AI models:

  • Automatic Self-Correction: When a build, test suite, or verification check fails during a workflow, Cosmogony automatically pinpoints the failure and repairs the issue in-flight before reaching your final sign-off gate.
  • Learns How Your Team Works—Without Model Retraining: Every checkpoint approval, human refinement, architectural preference, and verification outcome teaches Cosmogony how your team builds and reviews. The platform adapts dynamically across sessions with zero model retraining.
  • Compounding First-Pass Efficiency: By learning your team's conventions and eliminating recurring mistakes, Cosmogony steadily increases first-pass accuracy and drives down rework token waste over time.

Verified Results, Not Guesswork

"Done" in Cosmogony means the work has actually been built, tested, and verified in a clean workspace—not just that an AI claims it finished.

  • Automated quality checks confirm that the project builds cleanly and passes tests before the final checkpoint completes.
  • If a step runs into an issue, built-in One-Click Diagnostics pinpoint the cause and recommend the fastest way to resolve it.
  • A complete Workflow History & Tokenomics Ledger lets you inspect first-pass efficiency, self-correction passes, and document revisions at any time.

Collaborate Across Web, VS Code, Terminal & Mobile

Multiple teammates can join the same live session to follow progress, read deliverables, and collaborate in real time. Start a session from the Web dashboard, work alongside your code in the VS Code extension or Terminal UI, and review deliverables or approve checkpoints on the go from the Android mobile app.
