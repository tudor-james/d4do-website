Aligns to Your Policies and Processes

Cosmogony fits directly into how your organisation already builds, reviews, and ships—enhancing your process without replacing it.

Fits Seamlessly Into Your Delivery Pipeline

You bring your repositories, your CI/CD pipeline, your cloud environment, and your preferred AI models. Cosmogony works on an isolated session branch and prepares verified deliverables and pull requests. Your existing code review and release pipelines remain the final authority for everything that ships to production.

Cosmogony prepares the branch; your team owns the merge. Every session works on a dedicated feature branch (`cosmogony/<session-id>`). Your team reviews the deliverables and merges the pull request using your standard GitHub workflow.

Bring Your Own AI Models

Cosmogony is completely model-agnostic. Connect the AI providers your organisation has already approved—including OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Google Cloud Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, OpenRouter, or private local models via Ollama and LM Studio.

You can set sensible defaults at the organisation or project level, or choose specific models for individual sessions and workflow stages to balance speed, capability, and cost—with zero vendor lock-in.

Custom Workflow Studio

Every organisation has its own way of working. In addition to ready-made workflows for Software Delivery (RADPI), Enterprise Architecture (BAC4), and Security Threat Modeling (STARC), administrators can use the Workflow Studio to:

  • Inspect how each workflow stage and AI specialist team is structured.
  • Customize stage instructions, review gates, and AI model tiers to reflect your internal standards.
  • Publish immutable, versioned workflows so teams always run on a consistent, auditable process.

Real-Time Cost & Efficiency Visibility

Scaling AI across an engineering organisation requires clear visibility into return on investment. Cosmogony includes built-in Tokenomics & Efficiency Metrics across sessions and projects:

Stage-by-Stage Cost Breakdown

See exact token usage and cost across Requirements, Analysis, Design, Planning, Implementation, and Verification.

Run Efficiency Tracking

Measure how smoothly tasks progress through each stage and compare efficiency across runs and models.

Guided Setup Wizard

Onboard new organisations, teams, repositories, and AI providers in minutes through a single step-by-step wizard.

Direct GitHub Issue Integration

Browse open GitHub Issues inside Cosmogony and launch a scoped delivery session directly from an issue with one click.

Built for Regulated & Private Environments

All Cosmogony components can run within your own cloud or on-premises perimeter. Connect to private model endpoints in Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, or self-hosted servers, point dependency installs to internal package mirrors, and maintain a complete audit trail ready for ISO 27001 and enterprise compliance standards.
